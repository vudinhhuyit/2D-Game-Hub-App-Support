About

2D Game Hub is the home of a growing collection of small, hand-made 2D games for iPhone and iPad: platformers, puzzles, physics toys and more.

What you get

  • Quick, easy-to-learn games you can play in short sessions
  • Colorful pixel and hand-drawn art
  • No account needed; the games work offline
  • Free to play, with optional one-time upgrades in some games

New games are added over time. Check back soon!

Support

Need help with a game? Email vudinhhuyit@gmail.com and include:

  • The name of the game and its version
  • Your device model and iOS / iPadOS version
  • A short description of the problem (a screenshot helps)

How is my progress saved?

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting a game also deletes its unlocked levels and best scores.

How do I turn off sound?

Open the settings or pause menu inside the game and switch sound off.

I bought an upgrade but it's missing

Use the "Restore Purchases" button in the game. Purchases are handled by Apple and tied to your Apple ID.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This website

This page does not use cookies, trackers or analytics, and does not collect personal data. Your language choice is saved only in your browser.

Our apps

The games have no accounts and do not ask for your name, email or other personal details. We do not collect your game progress: it stays on your device. Depending on the game, these third-party services are used:

  • Google AdMob shows the ads. It may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events (for example a level started or finished) and device and app information to help us improve the games.
  • Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports with technical details such as device model, iOS version and the state of the app at the time of the crash, only to fix bugs.
  • Apple In-App Purchase – purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Not every game uses every service above.

Advertising and your choices

On first launch a game may ask, through the system “Allow tracking” dialog, whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), a consent form from Google lets you choose how ad data is used before ads are requested.

You can remove ads in games that offer a one-time Pro or Premium purchase. Learn more in Google’s policy on how it uses data from apps that use its services.

Data stored on your device

Progress, unlocked levels, best scores and stars, replay counts, purchases, and sound and language settings are stored on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app or use a game’s “Clear progress” option, where available.

Children's privacy

The games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob and limited by the settings above. Parents can remove all ads with a one-time purchase where a game offers one. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy
